Privacy Policy — Iron Proof

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Iron Proof is a habit tracker that builds a daily routine from your habits. It works entirely on your device. This policy describes what that means.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, health data or any other personal data.

Where your data lives

Your habits, check-ins, streaks, your routine (wake-up time, work hours, bedtime) and your Power score are stored inside the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us. We operate no server that receives them, and we cannot read them.

If your device backs itself up (for example iCloud Backup), the app's data may be included in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules notifications on your device at the times of your routine. They are created and delivered locally; nothing is sent through a server.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time purchase (PRO). Payment is handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your card details or billing address. The app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Apple ID, so it knows what to unlock.

Network connections

The app contacts the App Store to check the purchase and its price. No other network requests are made.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
