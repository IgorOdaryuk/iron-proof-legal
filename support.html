How does the app set the times?

You tell it when you wake up, when you work and when you go to bed. Each habit belongs to a part of the day (on waking, morning, afternoon, evening, before bed, or all day) and has a length. The app fits them around your work hours and packs your wind-down so it ends at bedtime. Change a habit's part of day, length or repeat days from its page; rebuild the whole day from the Path tab.

How does the timer work?

Tap the orange play button on a habit. A habit with a length counts down; finish the time and it is marked done, with the minutes logged. Stop early and the minutes still count, but the habit stays open. Habits without a length use a stopwatch.

How does the bookshelf work?

Open your reading habit and tap Bookshelf. Add the book you are reading — photograph its cover or pick a photo or screenshot. Time from your reading timer counts toward that book. Mark it finished and it joins your shelf.

I bought PRO on another device

Open the Path tab and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications for Iron Proof are allowed in the iPhone Settings app, and that reminders are on in your routine (Path → Rebuild my day). A habit already marked done today does not remind you again that day.

Is my data backed up?

Your data stays on your device and is included in your iPhone backup if you use one. There is no account and no cloud copy held by us.
